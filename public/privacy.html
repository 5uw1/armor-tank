Englishภาษาไทย
In short: Armor Tank does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Armor Tank ("the game") is published by suw1labs for Android, iOS, Windows and the web. This policy explains how the game handles information.

2. Information we collect

None. The game has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no crash-reporting services and no in-app purchases. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or device identifiers.

3. Data stored on your device

Your save slots (level progress, credits, experience, upgrades) and settings (language, sound, graphics) are stored only in the game's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

If you choose Export in the Save slots menu, the game creates a save file on your device. You decide where that file goes. Import only reads a file you select.

4. Network access

All game content is packaged with the app, and the game makes no network requests while you play. The Android app declares the standard Internet permission required by its app framework, but the game does not use it to send any data. The web version is downloaded from its hosting server like any website, and that server may keep standard access logs (such as IP address and time).

5. Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Third parties

The game includes no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app stores (Google Play, Apple App Store, Microsoft) may collect information under their own privacy policies when you download the game.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: suwijakza@gmail.com
